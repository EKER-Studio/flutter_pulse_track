PulseTrack is a professional heart rate monitor and workout analytics app for Bluetooth LE sensors.

Features:
• Real-time heart rate monitoring via BLE
• Live BPM waveform charts & skin contact detection
• Energy expenditure tracking (kcal / kJ)
• Workout history with heart rate zones Z1-Z5
• Health Connect and Apple Health sync
• CSV import/export and data management
• Adaptive navigation for phones and tablets
• Biometric protection and local-first encryption
• Home widgets and daily reminders

Privacy first: all data stays on your device, encrypted with AES-256.
